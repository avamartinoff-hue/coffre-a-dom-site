Privacy policy

A template aligned with the spirit of the Swiss FADP (revised FADP) and the GDPR, to be validated before going live.

Coffre à Dom values the protection of your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what your rights are.

1. Data controller

Coffre à Dom Sàrl (CHE-142.573.696), Route de Gouillon 10, 1898 St-Gingolph, Switzerland, coffreadom@hotmail.com.

2. Data we collect

  • Forms (contact, order): name, email, phone, message, order contents.
  • Newsletter: email address, managed through our email service provider Brevo.
  • Shop: when you make a purchase, order data is processed for preparation and shipping; payment is handled by our payment service provider (PSP), with card data never passing through our site.
  • Technical data: strictly necessary browsing data (see the cookie policy).

3. Purposes

  • Respond to your enquiries and process your reservations/orders.
  • Send you our newsletter, with your consent.
  • Ensure the proper operation and security of the site.

4. Legal basis

Your consent (newsletter, non-essential cookies), the performance of a contract (order), and our legitimate interest in operating and securing the site.

5. Service providers & data transfers

To provide the service, we use processors that may process data outside Switzerland/the EU, with appropriate safeguards:

  • Netlify (site hosting)
  • Supabase (database & customer accounts)
  • SumUp (secure bank card payment) and TWINT (mobile payment)
  • Brevo (newsletter and email delivery)

6. Retention period

Data is retained for as long as necessary for the purposes described, then deleted or anonymised. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time via the link included in every email.

7. Your rights

In accordance with the FADP and, where applicable, the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, object to and port your data. To exercise these rights, write to coffreadom@hotmail.com.

8. Security

We implement reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data.

9. Contact

For any question regarding this policy: coffreadom@hotmail.com.
