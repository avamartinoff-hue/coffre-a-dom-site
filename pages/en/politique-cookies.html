Cookie policy

How the site uses cookies and trackers — and how you stay in control.

A cookie is a small file placed on your device. We favour a respectful approach: by default, only strictly necessary cookies are active.

Strictly necessary cookies

Essential for the site to function — for example remembering the contents of your cart (stored locally on your device) or your cookie choice. They do not require consent.

Analytics & marketing cookies

These are only placed with your consent. They may come from our service providers when enabled:

  • SumUp — secure bank card payment.
  • Brevo — newsletter and communication.
  • Where applicable, a privacy-friendly audience measurement tool.

Third-party content

Some pages embed a map (OpenStreetMap) and web fonts. These services may place technical cookies when they load.

Manage your preferences

You can accept or refuse non-essential cookies via the banner shown on your first visit, and change your choice at any time below.

You can also delete cookies via your browser settings.
