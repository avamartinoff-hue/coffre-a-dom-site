About — Dom, that's me !

The story of one man… and a dream come true

Dom, that's me. But Dom is also a symbol : that of the Last Guardian, still standing to defend a world others have forgotten — the world of play, of sharing, of passion.

Born in the 90s, I grew up between Dragon Ball, Pokémon, video games and the cult adventure films that shaped my imagination and my taste for a challenge. Back then, my home was a little citadel : we traded cards, battled on consoles, made memories.

Today, that bond, that sacred fire… I refuse to let it die out.

The Coffre is :

A living shop

Pokémon cards, geek products, accessories…

A modern battlefield

Tournaments, evenings, challenges, competitions.

An open citadel

Events with figures from the scene like Benzol.

A tight-knit community

Players, collectors, the curious — all united by the same fire.

The Coffre has opened !

In 2021, I launched Coffre à Dom : a site dedicated to selling, to trading, but above all to passing on a passion. Then in 2023, the dream took shape : a physical shop, a gathering place, a sanctuary for players and collectors.

"I'm not just a seller. I'm a keeper of the flame."
